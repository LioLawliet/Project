Confess Web

A project featuring a confession-themed interface, with an animation of a text box dropping down along with a “Next” button to proceed to the following box. At the end, there are two buttons labeled “Yes” and “No,” each with its own unique effect. The background includes sliding heart emojis for visual decoration

Feature:

  • Interactive Yes/No Choice
  • Sliding Heart Emoji Background
  • Animations After a Choice is Selected
  • Confession-Themed Interface
